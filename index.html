FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering

What is the price of a slotted angle rack in Kathmandu?
Our racks start from NPR 3,000 for a standard 6ft × 3ft × 15" light-duty rack, and go up to NPR 25,000+ for heavy-duty industrial-grade racks. Use our instant quote calculator above or WhatsApp 9843653731 for exact pricing.
What is the load capacity per shelf?
We offer three categories: Light Duty (60-90 kg), Medium Duty (100-160 kg), and Heavy Duty (180-250+ kg) per shelf, depending on steel sheet and angle thickness. Use our calculator for precise estimates.
Do you deliver and install outside Kathmandu Valley?
Yes, we deliver across Nepal. Free delivery and installation is provided within Kathmandu Valley. For other cities (Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar, etc.), delivery charges apply based on weight and distance. Contact 9843653731 to confirm.
Can I customize the rack dimensions?
Absolutely. We manufacture every rack to order. You can choose height (4ft to 12ft), length, depth, shelf count, angle thickness, and colors. Just tell us your storage requirements.
Is there a warranty on the racks?
Yes. All racks come with a 2-year anti-rust warranty on powder coating and lifetime structural support. We use high-quality MS steel and industrial-grade powder coating.
Do you offer bulk order discounts?
Yes. We offer 3-6% off on orders above 5 sets and 6-10% off on orders above 10 sets. Contact us directly for large warehouse projects with 20+ racks for best rates.
