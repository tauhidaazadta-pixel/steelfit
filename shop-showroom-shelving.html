Shop Shelving FAQs

What is the best shelving for a retail shop in Kathmandu?
Slotted angle shelving with 0.7-0.8mm sheets is ideal for retail shops. It's lightweight, adjustable, and looks clean. For cosmetics and garments, 4-tier racks work well; for supermarkets, 5-6 tier racks with 100-120 kg per shelf.
Can I get custom colors for shop racks?
Yes. We powder coat in any RAL color — most popular for shops are white, black, blue, red, and brand-matching colors. Extra color customization takes 2-3 extra days.
How much does shop shelving cost in Nepal?
Retail shelving starts at NPR 4,000 per unit for a 4-tier display rack. Price depends on size, shelf count, and sheet thickness. Bulk shop fit-outs get special pricing.
Do you install in my shop?
Yes, free installation within Kathmandu Valley. Racks are usually assembled on-site in 30-45 minutes per unit. Evening installations (after shop closing) available by request.
