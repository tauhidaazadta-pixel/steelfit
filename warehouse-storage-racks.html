Built for Maximum Space Utilization

Warehouses in Kathmandu face a common challenge — limited floor space with growing inventory. Our heavy-duty warehouse storage racks solve this by maximizing vertical storage using industrial-grade slotted angle steel structures engineered for real warehouse loads.

Each rack is manufactured in our Balkhu workshop using 40×40mm or 60×40mm MS slotted angles in 2.0mm-2.5mm thickness, paired with reinforced steel shelves. Whether you store sacks of grain, automotive parts, electronics cartons, garment bundles, or bulk construction materials, we customize the rack dimensions, load capacity, and shelf count to fit your exact warehouse layout.

All racks are powder coated using multi-stage pre-treatment and industrial-grade powder coating that resists rust, moisture, and dust — critical for Nepal's monsoon season. Our warehouse clients across Balaju, Balaju Industrial District, Hetauda, Bhaktapur, Lalitpur, and Birgunj rely on our racks for their daily operations.

Warehouse Rack FAQs

What is the maximum load capacity per shelf?
Our warehouse-grade racks support 180-250+ kg per shelf depending on angle and sheet thickness. For bulk storage we recommend 2.0mm angles with 1.0mm or 1.2mm sheets with center stiffener channels.
What is the price of a warehouse rack in Kathmandu?
Racks start at NPR 8,000 for medium-duty and go up to NPR 35,000+ for extra-heavy multi-tier industrial racks. Bulk orders (10+ sets) get 6-10% discount.
How many shelf levels can I add?
We build racks with 4 to 7 shelf levels based on ceiling height. For most warehouses with 10-12ft ceilings, 5-6 shelves provide optimal space utilization with proper bottom clearance.
Do you deliver outside Kathmandu?
Yes. Free delivery & installation within Kathmandu Valley. For Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar, Birgunj, Butwal and other cities, delivery charges apply based on weight and distance.
