Organize Your Documents Professionally

Paperwork is still the backbone of business in Nepal — from loan files in banks to employee records in HR departments to legal archives in law firms. But scattered documents cost time, money, and productivity every single day. Our office and archive file racks solve this with purpose-built storage that keeps every file accessible, labeled, and organized.

Our file racks are designed for practical office realities — 15" depth fits standard A4 files, box files, and binders perfectly. Adjustable shelf heights let you accommodate tall ledger books or small archive boxes without wasted space. The open-shelf design means no doors to open, no keys to manage — just quick visual access to what you need.

Each rack is powder coated in standard grey, white, or black — clean and professional, suitable for reception-adjacent areas as well as back offices. All racks are modular and can be disassembled and reinstalled if your office shifts.

File Rack FAQs

What is the best file rack for office document storage?
For offices we recommend 4-5 tier racks with 15" depth and 0.7mm sheets. They handle standard A4 files, box files, and binders. For record rooms with high volume, 6-tier racks with 18" depth offer better density.
Do you make fire-resistant file racks?
Our standard steel racks are not fire-rated, but steel construction resists fire far better than wood. For critical documents we recommend adding fireproof cabinet inserts — we can custom-build these on request.
What is the price of office file racks in Nepal?
Office file racks start at NPR 3,500 per unit for a 4-tier rack. Bulk orders for banks and government offices get special pricing. Call 9843653731 for bulk quote.
Can I move the racks later?
Yes. Our racks are modular — they bolt together and can be disassembled and relocated easily. Ideal for offices planning to shift or expand.
